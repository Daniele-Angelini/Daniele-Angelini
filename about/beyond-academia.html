Beyond academia

Experiences outside university life

Academic work is only one part of my life. This page is a quieter space for experiences, activities and interests that have contributed to the way I work with people, organise projects and approach education.

Scouting

Education, responsibility and community

Scouting has been an important experience beyond my academic activity. It brings together education, responsibility, teamwork, planning and life outdoors, and offers a very different environment in which to learn how groups grow and work together.

This section can gradually become a record of the experiences, projects and responsibilities that I want to make public, without turning the website into a private diary.

Why include it here?

Because some formative experiences are not captured by an academic CV, even when they strongly influence the way we teach, collaborate and take responsibility.
