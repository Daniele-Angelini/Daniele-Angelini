About me

I am a researcher in mathematical finance with a background in theoretical physics. My work is centred on stochastic processes, fractional modelling, volatility and statistical methods for irregular and persistent dynamics.

This page is a personal gateway. The stable part tells the story of my academic path; the dynamic part collects occasional stories, photographs and notes from academic life and beyond.
