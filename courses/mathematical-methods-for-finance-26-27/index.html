Programme and learning outcomes Six modules, two bridge lessons and one final examination workshop 21 lessons
Learning outcomes What students should be able to do by the end
  • Work with metric, normed, Banach and Hilbert spaces.
  • Use measure, integration and convergence theorems.
  • Analyse random variables and conditional expectations.
  • Distinguish modes of convergence and use LLN and CLT.
  • Work with processes, filtrations, martingales and Brownian motion.
  • Compute stochastic integrals and apply Itô's formula.
  • Use measure changes, SDEs and Feynman–Kac formulas.
  • Present solutions clearly, rigorously and efficiently.
Bridge Preparatory bridge23–24 September · 2 lessons

Topics: notation, calculus, vectors, matrices, row-by-column multiplication, linear independence, probability, expectation and variance.

Purpose: establish a common starting point and identify gaps early.

1 Functional analysis30 September–7 October · 3 lessons

Core: metric spaces, convergence, completeness, Baire category, compactness, Heine–Borel, vector and normed spaces, Hölder and Minkowski inequalities, Banach and Hilbert spaces, projection, orthonormal systems, Bessel inequality and linear operators.

Spaces: finite- and infinite-dimensional spaces, sequence, function, continuous, smooth, Schwartz and Hölder spaces.

2 Measure, integration and Fourier analysis8–15 October · 3 lessons

Core: sigma-algebras, Borel sets, measures, measurable functions, Lebesgue integral, integrability, equivalence classes, MCT, Fatou, DCT, product measures, Fubini–Tonelli and Lp spaces.

Fourier: trigonometric polynomials, Fourier series, transform, Plancherel theorem, convolution and FFT.

3 Probability and limit theorems21–28 October · 3 lessons

Core: probability spaces, random variables, laws, expectation, Jensen, variance, covariance, correlation, independence, Fubini, conditional laws and expectation, Radon–Nikodym and Bayes.

Limits: Markov inequality, modes of convergence, LLN, CLT, characteristic functions, inversion, weak convergence and Lévy continuity.

Methods: Cholesky decomposition, conditional marginals and Least Squares Monte Carlo.

4 Stochastic processes and martingales29 October–5 November · 3 lessons

Core: process laws, finite-dimensional distributions, measurable processes, filtrations, martingales, stopping times, Markov and Feller processes, Brownian motion, Wiener space and Poisson processes.

Dynamics: Chapman–Kolmogorov, semigroups, generators, forward and backward Kolmogorov equations, continuous and càdlàg martingale classes.

5 Stochastic integration and Itô calculus11–18 November · 3 lessons

Core: variation, Riemann–Stieltjes integration, semimartingales, Doob decomposition, quadratic variation, indicator and simple integrands, L2 and local L2 construction, Itô processes and Itô's formula.

Extensions: continuous semimartingales, multidimensional calculus and Lévy characterisation.

6 Measure changes, SDEs and Feynman–Kac19–26 November · 3 lessons

Core: changes of measure, density processes, exponential martingales, Novikov and Kazamaki conditions, Girsanov theorem, Brownian martingale representation, SDEs and Feynman–Kac formulas.

Final Consolidation and mock examination2 December · 1 lesson

Complete programme map, mock written examination, public correction and clinic of recurring errors. No new material is introduced.
